FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Contact us.

Does it cost anything?

No. Collection is free for households, and we never charge donors.

Do you pay for e-waste?

No. TechSeva is a nonprofit service, not a scrap buyer. Our job is to make sure your e-waste is recycled responsibly.

Which areas do you cover?

Kolkata and nearby areas. If you’re further away, contact us anyway and we may be able to arrange a drive.

Is there a minimum quantity?

No. For one or two small items, dropping off at a drive is usually easiest, but just ask.

Do you accept working devices?

Yes. Working devices in good condition may be passed on for reuse where safe; everything else is recycled.

Can I donate batteries?

Intact ones, yes. Tape the terminals of loose batteries. We can’t accept swollen or leaking batteries, or vehicle batteries.

Is my data safe?

Please back up and factory-reset devices before handover. Our data-wipe guide shows how. Wiping it yourself first is always the best protection.

Who recycles the e-waste?

Hulladek Recycling, a Kolkata-based recycler authorised by the Central Pollution Control Board.

How do I know it’s actually recycled?

Each consignment gets an official certificate, published on our Impact page.

Can my organisation get a certificate?

Yes. For organisations and large donations we share the certificate covering your consignment.

Have old electronics at home?

Tell us what you have. We’ll arrange a free pickup and make sure it’s recycled properly.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about donating, data safety, recycling and partnering with TechSeva.

General

What is TechSeva?
TechSeva is a student-led nonprofit initiative in Kolkata that collects e-waste from households, schools and businesses and sends it to a government-authorised recycler for safe processing.
Does it cost anything?
No. Collection is free for households, and we never charge donors. For very large commercial volumes we may discuss logistics with you, but we’ll always say so upfront.
Do you pay for e-waste?
No. TechSeva is a nonprofit service, not a scrap buyer. Our job is to make sure your e-waste is recycled responsibly, not to resell it.
Which areas do you cover?
We currently serve Kolkata and nearby areas. If you’re further away, contact us anyway. We may be able to arrange a drive or point you to an authorised collection centre.

Donating

What items do you accept?
Almost anything with a plug, battery or circuit board, from phones, laptops and cables to monitors, small appliances and ACs. See the full list.
Is there a minimum quantity for pickup?
No strict minimum. For a single charger or phone it’s usually easiest to drop it off at our next drive, but just ask and we’ll work something out.
Do you accept devices that still work?
Yes. Working devices in good condition may be passed on for reuse where that’s safe. Everything else is recycled.
Can I donate batteries?
Intact batteries, yes. Keep them inside the device, or tape the terminals of loose cells and pack them separately. We can’t accept swollen, leaking or damaged batteries, or vehicle batteries.
How soon can you pick up?
Usually within a week of your request. Large appliances may need a little more time to arrange transport.

Data & recycling

Is my data safe?
Please back up, sign out of all accounts and factory-reset your devices before handing them over. Our data-safety guide shows how. Our recycling partner also offers data destruction as part of its process, but wiping your device first is always the best protection.
Who recycles the e-waste?
Hulladek Recycling, a Kolkata-based e-waste management company authorised by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB).
How do I know it’s actually recycled?
Every consignment receives an official recycling certificate from our partner. We publish them on our Impact page.
Can I get a certificate for my donation?
For organisations and large donations, ask us and we’ll share the consignment certificate covering your items. Individuals can get an email confirmation of what was collected.

Yes, we’d love that. See Host a drive or get in touch.

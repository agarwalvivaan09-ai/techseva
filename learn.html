The basics

The fastest-growing waste stream

E-waste is any discarded product with a plug, battery or circuit board. Worldwide it is growing about five times faster than documented recycling. Much of India’s e-waste is still handled informally through open burning and acid baths, which exposes workers and neighbourhoods to toxic fumes.

The same devices also contain copper, gold and rare metals that proper recycling can recover. That’s why e-waste is often called an “urban mine”.

The law in India

E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022

In force from 1 April 2023. The rules place Extended Producer Responsibility on manufacturers and require e-waste to be processed only by registered recyclers. Consumers should hand e-waste to authorised channels, not informal scrap dealers.

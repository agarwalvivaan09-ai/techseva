Why it matters

Every kilo kept out of the informal sector counts.

Much of India’s e-waste is handled informally, through open burning, acid baths and crude dismantling that expose workers and neighbourhoods to toxic fumes and heavy metals. Routing devices to an authorised recycler means hazardous parts are treated safely and valuable materials are recovered.

Our goal for the coming year is to run regular drives with schools, housing societies and local businesses, and to cross 1,000 kg of certified recycling.

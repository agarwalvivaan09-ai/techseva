E-waste collection · Kolkata

Responsible recycling for the electronics you no longer use.

TechSeva collects old and broken electronics from homes, schools and offices and hands every kilo to a government-authorised recycler. It’s free, and every consignment is certified.

Process

From your drawer to certified recycling

01

Request

Use the pickup form or call us with a rough list of items.

02

Prepare

Back up and wipe your devices, and remove SIM and memory cards.

03

Collect

We pick up from your door, or you drop off at a drive.

04

Recycle

A CPCB-authorised recycler processes it and issues a certificate.

What we accept

If it has a plug, battery or circuit board, send it our way.

Phones, laptops, monitors, cables, chargers, printers, routers, small kitchen appliances, and even ACs and fridges by arrangement. Working or broken.

See the full list

Toxic if dumped

Lead, mercury and cadmium leach into soil and water.

Valuable if recycled

Copper, gold and rare metals are recovered and reused.

Handled safely

Data-safety guidance and an authorised recycler only.

Fully documented

Every consignment is weighed and certified.

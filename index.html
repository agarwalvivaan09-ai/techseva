The problem

The world’s fastest-growing waste stream is sitting in our drawers.

In 2022 the world generated a record 62 million tonnes of e-waste, and only 22.3% of it was documented as properly collected and recycled.

Old phones, chargers and cables hold lead, mercury, cadmium and flame retardants that leak into soil and water when dumped or burned. They also hold copper, gold and other valuable metals that can be recovered and reused. TechSeva closes that gap, starting where the problem starts: our own homes.

Learn about e-waste

Toxic if dumped

Heavy metals and chemicals pollute land, water and air when e-waste is burned or landfilled.

Valuable if recycled

Copper, aluminium, gold and rare metals can be recovered and returned to manufacturing.

Data at risk

Old phones and drives often still hold personal data. They need careful handling.

Stored indefinitely

Most households keep unused gadgets for years because they don’t know where to send them.

How it works

Four steps from clutter to certified recycling

We’ve kept it simple. You tell us what you have, and we handle the rest.

Tell us

Fill in the pickup form or call us with a rough list of items and your location.

Prepare

Back up and wipe your data, remove SIM and memory cards, and bag small items together.

We collect

We pick up at a time that suits you, or you drop off at a collection drive.

Certified recycling

Everything goes to Hulladek Recycling, a CPCB-authorised recycler, and we receive a recycling certificate.

About TechSeva

Small, local, and fully accountable.

TechSeva (seva means service) began as a student-led effort to collect e-waste from family, friends and neighbours. It has grown into a repeatable system of outreach, collection, logistics and certified recycling.

Every drive is weighed and documented, and every kilo is backed by a certificate from our recycling partner.

How it works

Donating e-waste takes about ten minutes.

Follow these steps and general guidelines to make your donation quick, safe and secure.

Request

Fill in the pickup form, email or call us. Share a rough list of items, approximate quantity and your area.

Prepare

Back up and wipe your devices, remove SIM and memory cards, and bag small items together (see the checklist below).

Hand over

We confirm a pickup slot, usually within a week, or share the date and venue of the next drop-off drive.

Recycled

Items are weighed and sent to our CPCB-authorised partner. On request, we email you a confirmation of what was collected.

General instructions

Before you hand over your e-waste

  • Back up anything you want to keep: photos, contacts, documents.
  • Sign out of all accounts (Google, Apple ID, Samsung, iCloud) and turn off “Find My Device”.
  • Factory reset phones, tablets and laptops. See the data-safety guide below.
  • Remove SIM cards, memory cards and any discs from drives.
  • Tape battery terminals on loose batteries and keep them in a separate bag.
  • Bundle cables and chargers together with a rubber band or tie.
  • Bag small items together in a box or cloth bag. Please avoid plastic where you can.
  • Don’t dismantle devices yourself. Screens and batteries can be hazardous.
  • Keep large appliances unplugged and accessible for pickup.

Data safety guide

You are responsible for wiping your personal data before donating. Here’s how:

DeviceWhat to do
Android phoneSettings → System → Reset options → Erase all data (factory reset). Remove your Google account first.
iPhone / iPadSign out of Apple ID, then Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Erase All Content and Settings.
Windows PCSettings → System → Recovery → Reset this PC → Remove everything, with the “clean data” option.
MacSystem Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Erase All Content and Settings.
Dead deviceIf it won’t power on, tell us. You can remove the hard drive or keep it, and we’ll flag it for secure handling.
Tip: menu names vary slightly by brand and OS version. If you’re stuck, call us and we’ll walk you through it.
After collection

What happens to your e-waste

Sorted & weighed

We categorise the items and record total weight for each consignment.

Transported

The consignment goes to Hulladek Recycling, an authorised recycler.

Dismantled

Devices are taken apart and hazardous parts are isolated for safe treatment.

Recovered

Metals, plastics and glass are recovered, and a recycling certificate is issued.

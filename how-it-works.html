How it works

Donating takes about ten minutes.

Request a pickup, prepare your devices with the checklist below, and we handle the rest.

General instructions

Before you hand over

  • Back up photos, contacts and documents.
  • Sign out of Google / Apple ID and turn off Find My Device.
  • Factory reset phones, tablets and laptops.
  • Remove SIM cards, memory cards and discs.
  • Tape terminals of loose batteries and bag them separately.
  • Bundle cables and put small items in one bag or box.
  • Don’t dismantle devices. Screens and batteries can be hazardous.

Get involved

Help us collect more, recycle better, reach further.

Whether you have an afternoon, a classroom, a housing society or a company, there’s a way to be part of TechSeva.

Volunteer with us

Help with outreach, sorting and weighing at drives, social media, design or logistics. Students are especially welcome, and we can provide a volunteering certificate for your hours.

Sign up to volunteer

Host a collection drive

Bring TechSeva to your housing society, school, college, club or place of worship. We provide posters, guidelines and on-the-day support, and handle the recycling.

Plan a drive

Schools & colleges

Run a campus e-waste drive paired with a short awareness session for students. It works well for eco-clubs, community-service hours and Environment Day events.

Businesses & offices

Clear out old IT equipment, cables and appliances responsibly, as we did with Delight Dairy. Ask us about organising a collection at your premises.

Hosting a drive

How a TechSeva drive works

Pick a date & venue

A society clubhouse, school lobby or office reception works well. Two to three hours is usually enough.

Spread the word

We share a ready-made poster and WhatsApp message with our guidelines one week ahead.

Collect & weigh

Our team receives, sorts and weighs items on the day.

Certified recycling

We send the consignment to our recycler and share the final weight with you.

Get involved

Help us collect more and reach further.

Volunteer, host a drive, or bring TechSeva to your school or office.

Volunteer

Help with outreach, sorting at drives, design or logistics. Students can receive a volunteering certificate.

Schools & societies

Host a drop-off drive. We provide posters and on-the-day support, and handle the recycling.

Businesses

Clear out old IT equipment and appliances responsibly, as Delight Dairy did.

Hosting a drive

How a drive works

  • Pick a date and venue. A clubhouse, school lobby or office reception for 2–3 hours.
  • Spread the word. We share a ready-made poster and WhatsApp message a week ahead.
  • Collect and weigh. Our team receives and sorts items on the day.
  • Certified recycling. We share the final weight with you.

About

Service to the planet, one drawer at a time.

Seva means service. TechSeva is a student-led nonprofit that makes responsible e-waste disposal easy for households and communities in Kolkata.

Origin

It started with a drawer.

While cleaning his room, founder Vivaan Agarwal found a drawer full of old cables, broken devices and unused electronics that had sat there for years. When he asked whether he could just throw it out, he learned that e-waste can be seriously harmful if it isn’t disposed of properly.

If one drawer held this much, so did thousands of others. TechSeva grew from that idea: collect e-waste directly from local households and make sure every kilo is recycled responsibly.

Mission

Make responsible e-waste disposal the default for every home, school and office in our community through free collection, clear guidance and verified recycling.

Vision

A city where no gadget ends up in a landfill, a drain or an open fire, and recovered materials go back into use.

Principles

How we operate

Transparency

Every consignment is weighed, and every certificate is published.

Responsibility

Authorised recyclers only, never informal scrap dealers.

Community

We start with the people around us: families, neighbours, schools and local businesses.

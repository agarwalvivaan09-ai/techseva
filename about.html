About us

Service to the planet, one drawer at a time.

TechSeva is a student-led nonprofit initiative that makes responsible e-waste disposal easy for households and communities in Kolkata.

Our origin

The idea started with a drawer.

While cleaning his room, founder Vivaan Agarwal opened a drawer filled with old cables, broken devices and unused electronics that had sat there for years. It looked like clutter. When he asked whether he could just throw it all out, he learned that e-waste can be seriously harmful if it isn’t disposed of properly.

That moment changed his perspective. If one drawer held this much e-waste, so did thousands of others. What looked like a small personal problem was part of a much larger one.

That realisation became TechSeva: an initiative to collect e-waste directly from local households and make sure it is responsibly recycled.

Key insight

Environmental impact doesn’t have to begin at scale. It begins with a problem in your own surroundings, and a process that turns awareness into action.

Our mission

To make responsible e-waste disposal the default for every household, school and office in our community by removing the friction: free collection, clear guidance and verified recycling.

Our vision

A city where no gadget ends up in a landfill, a drain or an open fire, and where recovered materials go back into the economy instead of polluting it.

What we do

Four pillars of every TechSeva drive

Outreach

We reach out to households and communities and help people find the unused electronics they’ve forgotten about.

Collection

We collect e-waste directly from homes, societies and offices, or through drop-off drives.

Coordination

We sort, weigh and coordinate safe transport of everything collected to our recycling partner.

Recycling

An authorised recycler dismantles and processes the material and issues a certificate for each consignment.

Our values

What we stand for

Transparency

Every consignment is weighed, and every recycling certificate is published on our Impact page.

Responsibility

We work only with recyclers authorised under India’s E-Waste (Management) Rules. No informal scrap dealers, ever.

Community

We start with the people around us: families, neighbours, schools and local businesses.

Founder

Vivaan Agarwal

Vivaan is a student in Kolkata with interests in economics, finance and entrepreneurship. He founded TechSeva to turn a personal realisation into a working system with measurable outcomes. He leads its outreach, partnerships and operations.

Want to collaborate, invite TechSeva to your school or society, or just ask a question? He’d love to hear from you.

Our recycling partner

Hulladek Recycling is a Kolkata-based e-waste management company authorised by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). It handles collection, dismantling and recycling of electronic waste, along with data destruction. Each TechSeva consignment is backed by a Hulladek recycling certificate.

What we accept

Working or broken, we take it.

Almost anything with a plug, battery or circuit board. Here’s the list, plus the few items we can’t handle.

Computing

  • Laptops, desktops, CPUs
  • Keyboards, mice, webcams
  • Hard drives, pen drives
  • Routers and modems

Mobile & personal

  • Phones and tablets
  • Smartwatches
  • Headphones, speakers
  • Cameras, consoles

Screens

  • LCD / LED monitors
  • Televisions
  • Projectors
  • Set-top boxes, remotes

Cables & power

  • Chargers and adapters
  • Cables of all kinds
  • Extension boards
  • Power banks (terminals taped)

Small appliances

  • Microwaves, OTGs, UV cabinets
  • Mixers, kettles, toasters
  • Irons, hair dryers
  • Fans and heaters

Large appliances

  • Window and split ACs
  • Refrigerators
  • Washing machines
  • Call ahead to arrange transport
Not accepted

Please keep these out

  • Swollen, leaking or damaged batteries
  • Vehicle and large lead-acid batteries
  • Broken CFLs and tube lights (they contain mercury)
  • Contaminated medical devices
  • Non-electronic waste: plastic, paper, furniture, clothes
Battery safety. Lithium-ion batteries can catch fire if punctured. Leave intact batteries inside the device, or tape the terminals of loose ones and pack them separately.

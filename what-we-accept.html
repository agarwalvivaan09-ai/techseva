What we accept

What can I give to TechSeva?

Almost anything with a plug, battery or circuit board. Here’s the full list, plus the few things we can’t take.

IT & computing

  • Laptops & desktops / CPU cabinets
  • Keyboards, mice, webcams
  • Hard drives, SSDs, pen drives
  • Motherboards, RAM, graphics cards
  • Routers, modems, switches

Mobile & personal

  • Mobile & feature phones
  • Tablets & e-readers
  • Smartwatches & fitness bands
  • Earphones, headphones, speakers
  • Cameras & gaming consoles

Screens & display

  • LCD / LED monitors
  • LED & LCD televisions
  • Old CRT TVs & monitors (by arrangement)
  • Projectors
  • Set-top boxes & remotes

Cables & power

  • Chargers & adapters
  • All kinds of cables & wires
  • Extension boards
  • Power banks (terminals taped)
  • UPS & inverters (by arrangement)

Small appliances

  • Microwaves, OTGs, UV cabinets
  • Mixers, toasters, kettles
  • Irons, hair dryers, trimmers
  • Fans & electric heaters
  • Electronic toys

Large appliances

  • Air conditioners (window & split)
  • Refrigerators
  • Washing machines
  • Water purifiers
  • Please call ahead so we can arrange transport
What we can’t take

Please keep these out of your e-waste bag

These items need specialised handling that our collection drives aren’t equipped for. We’re happy to point you to the right place.

  • Swollen, leaking or damaged batteries. These are a fire risk in transit.
  • Car / vehicle batteries and large lead-acid batteries.
  • Broken CFL bulbs and tube lights (they contain mercury).
  • Medical devices with biological contamination.
  • Non-electronic waste: plastic, paper, glass, furniture, clothes.
  • Gas cylinders, paint, chemicals or any hazardous liquids.
Batteries: handle with care.

Lithium-ion batteries (in phones, laptops and power banks) can catch fire if punctured or short-circuited. Leave them inside the device if they’re intact, or tape over the terminals of loose batteries and pack them separately. Never put a swollen battery in a bag with other items.

Working vs. broken

Does it matter if it still works?

No, we accept both. Working devices in good condition may be passed on for reuse where it’s safe to do so. Everything else is recycled.

Got a drawer full of old electronics?

Tell us what you have. We’ll arrange a free pickup or drop-off and make sure it’s recycled responsibly.
